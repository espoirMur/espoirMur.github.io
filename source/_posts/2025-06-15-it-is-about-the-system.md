# To the Developers on the Other Side of the World: It's Not You, It's the System  

unfortunately job application after:2021/1/1 before:2023/1/1 

This is a long-overdue post. For years, I wanted to share this, but the stress of day-to-day survival kept me silent. Today, I'm writing for every developer who's been rejected, ghosted, or told *"unfortunately"* after hundreds of applications.  

**This is not about your skills.**  
It's about where you were born.  

When a hiring manager asks, *"Do you have the right to work in the UK?"* and you say **No**, you're disqualified. Not because you're unqualified—but because they don't want the hassle of sponsorship, or the UK government forces them to pay a £5,000+ skills surcharge.

## The System  

### 1. The Young Boy from Congo  
I graduated in Bukavu, fled to Goma for opportunity, then applied *7 times* to Andela Kenya and *2 times* to Andela Uganda. Rejected every time. Later, I learned the truth: **They prioritized locals.**  

When I finally joined Andela Rwanda, I saw Congolese developers rejected because the Rwandan government funded training for *their* citizens.  

This is the ugly truth: **Countries protect their own.** Why hire a foreigner when locals need jobs?  

### 2. The UK Illusion  
I made it to London—the financial capital. But here's the reality:  
- International students pay £10,000+ for degrees, lured by the "Graduate Visa."  
- Recruiters ghost you the second they hear "sponsorship."  
- The government claims to want "global talent," but companies won't jump through hoops.

### 3. The Remote Work Lie  
*"Just work remotely!"* they say. But why would a country let you earn their currency and spend it elsewhere?  

In London, your salary fuels the economy:  
- Rent → Landlords win  
- Transport → TfL wins  
- Coffee, lunches, taxes → The UK wins  

The whole London lives on money for office worker.

## How to Fight Back  
If the system won't change, **outsmart it.**  

1. **Master the Fundamentals**  
   - Ignore the "AI will replace devs" noise. Companies like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Bloomberg are *desperate* for skilled engineers.  

2. **Target Global Companies**  
   - FAANG, Bloomberg, and Microsoft hire from Africa. Prep for leetcode, contribute to open-source, and network relentlessly.  
   - **PLACEHOLDER:** *[Add a link to Bloomberg's Lagos recruitment drive.]*  

3. **Leverage Academia**  
   - Scholarships (Chevening, DAAD) can be your ticket out. Aim for Germany, Canada, or Australia if the UK locks you out.  

4. **Build a Portfolio That Speaks for You**  
   - A GitHub with 10k stars bypasses "right to work" questions.

You weren't born in the "right" country? **Fine.** Outwork the system until it has no choice but to see you.
