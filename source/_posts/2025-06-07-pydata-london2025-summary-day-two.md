I’ve always looked forward to great conferences—events where I get to meet brilliant people in the tech industry and catch up with recent advancements in the NLP field. This year, I attended PyData London 2025. Yes, it’s PyData—a conference for everyone who works in the data field using Python. As you may have guessed, this year it wasn’t about Pandas vs. Polars; I think we’ve moved away from that. Now, it’s all about LLMs and how everyone is building applications around them.

In this post, I’ll highlight some of the talks I attended on Saturday during the conference. I’ll discuss two presentations that caught my attention and the lessons learned, then mention other noteworthy talks.
